# Reconciliation Problem: The Amount Ledger Needs Re-reading

**Human Cognome Project, 29 August 2026. For the ledger and field packages.**

## The problem

Every amount on the Mann ledger was taken through a sampler with a cycle. A source that is present on every cycle registers at the sampler's full scale whatever the cycle is. Read through the weld, that full-scale registration was recorded as a high frequency and converted to a high energy. Heat is the type case: it sits near the top of the old ledger because it never stops arriving, not because it carries a large amount per event. Separating frequency from power makes this visible. It does not fix it, because the amount was never read on its own.

The mechanics going in: **periodicity is not inherent; it develops.** In a monopolar environment, one centre in a continuous field, relaxation is radial and no period exists anywhere in it. In any polycentric environment periodicity must develop, because the centres' emission patterns meet at positions fixed by their geometry and the propagation rate; the meeting pattern is stationary in space (Young 1804) and periodic in time as soon as anything moves relative to anything else (Rayleigh 1877). The period is the timetable of the collisions, not a property of any centre. Adjustment and counter-adjustment between two centres pass back and forth with a period set by the distance and propagation time between them. Kepler's third law is this statement for orbits (period from separation alone); a cavity mode is the same statement for a bounded field (period from the round trip across the boundary). Nothing is periodic by itself. A periodic emission is signal-equivalent: a repeating exchange is a created waveform series that a sampler can lock to. A continuous emission has no exchange behind it, no series to lock to, and fills every window.

## What has to be measured

Across the electromagnetic spectrum, with the sampler's cycle **fixed and stated**:

1. **Amount registered per cycle** in each band.
2. **Continuity.** Is the source present on every cycle (aperiodic, radial, force-like) or on a pattern of cycles (periodic, signal-equivalent)?
3. **Cycle dependence.** At what sampler cycles does the registered amount change? A continuous source saturates at every cycle; a periodic source registers only when the cycle resolves its pattern.

Repeat at several fixed cycles. The result is an amount ledger with the sampler declared on every entry, and a continuity flag on every entry.

## What the record already contains

Radio astronomy distinguishes **continuum emission** from **line emission** by integration time and bandwidth (Condon and Ransom, *Essential Radio Astronomy*, Princeton 2016, ch. 2 and 7). Continuum is the aperiodic case; line emission is the periodic case. That distinction is the one this problem needs, and it is applied routinely without being read against a fixed-cycle amount ledger. Thermal (free-free) and synchrotron continuum, hydrogen recombination lines, the 21 cm line, and masers are all archived with integration times recorded.

COBE FIRAS sampled the cosmic background at stepped path differences and Fourier-transformed the record (Fixsen et al. 1996, ApJ 473:576). Its frequency axis is the instrument's decomposition of a continuous input; its integration per sample is in the archive.

Time-domain astronomy (pulsar timing, fast radio bursts, variability surveys) is the periodic case sampled at declared cadences (Lorimer and Kramer, *Handbook of Pulsar Astronomy*, 2005).

So the re-measurement partly exists. What does not exist is the reading: the archived amounts have not been placed on a ledger where the sampler's cycle is the declared unit and continuity is the first flag.

## For a specialist to compare

- Take any archived continuum measurement and any archived line measurement in the same band with recorded integration times. Express each amount per declared cycle. Does the continuum entry register at the instrument's full scale for every integration time, and the line entry only at integration times that resolve it?
- On the Mann ledger the three thermal entries (2.7 K, 310 K, 5772 K) sit at 6.1 × 10³, 6.5 × 10⁴ and 2.8 × 10⁵ Mann. Those positions are rational. Their amounts are the saturation levels of thermometry. What does each read as an amount per declared cycle?
- The twelve measured amounts on the ledger (four chemical, six particle, two thermal, with the cosmic background making three thermal) each came through a specific instrument. For each, was the source continuous or patterned during the read, and was the instrument's cycle stated?

## Expected outcome

An amount ledger in which every entry carries a sampler cycle and a continuity flag, and in which continuous sources are recorded as amount-per-cycle rather than as a frequency. On such a ledger the forces (aperiodic) and the interactions (often periodic) separate by flag, and the power scale can be rebuilt from reads rather than from conversion.
