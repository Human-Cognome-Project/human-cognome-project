## Target terminology for #324

At the working LoD, structural composition is settled for the calculation. The parent fields exposed by the immediately coarser level carry their subtree contributions down to a common base. Inspect a finer level to observe how its confounding contributions become part of the coarser object’s structure. Use the retained masses for initial observations. Record any temporary mass adjustment with the original value, override and exposure.

The contributions inherited from parents, together with the organization that makes the assembly a particle at its working LoD, define the shape of its possible manifold. Confounding fields establish attractors within that manifold. Nibble-level structure recurs across the whole space. A higher-order centroid applies wherever its more specific organization occurs. This describes how widely a field can participate. A shared nibble can act through the assembled particle’s represented share. Direct physical contact also requires scope and range at the active exposure. [Worked nibble example](../parent-structure-notes.md#the-unicode-hex-example)

Patrick’s point-centre proposal gives each occurrence a location and each participating field an effective span. Several occurrences may cluster tightly while their combined influence reaches farther. Their containers preserve occurrence identity even at equal published coordinates. The proposed coincidence test compares all three coordinates at the working precision. The span calculation and treatment of exact coincidence are under investigation. [Current assignment](../core-local-study-continuation-plan.md#investigate-field-span-and-point-coincidence)

The **structural target** is derived from parent/object classification forces along the composition path. Parent values aggregate upward and establish the coherence frame. The **confounded target** incorporates all participating fields, including the structural contributions. Contextual fields act within the degrees of freedom supplied by that structure. Either target can move, and the two may coincide.

At the working level, parent fields and core whole-particle fields are structural. Effects across these structures are confounding or transitive. For letters, each endpoint binds its ordered Unicode character parent or parents. Each character in turn has its own construction. Sharing a core definition supplies a possible path between endpoints. The imposed cross-language comparison determines whether an effect along that path participates. [Language-to-core construction](../language-component-unicode-plan.md).

Use *centroid* for an actual geometric or mass-weighted centre. Keep the proposed endpoint and accepted tick position distinct from both targets. The existing combined diagnostic retains its source-defined meaning. The confounded target is the final all-fields target calculation for the tick. The brake acts only on motion relative to that target. For fixed pre-tick inputs, braking preserves both calculated targets. ## Activity, focus and privacy

Use fork-native activation/deactivation and traversal before custom machinery.
One binary activity set per particle type selects work; type determines phase.
Particle calculation/consumption precedes contact activity propagation, then
centroid propagation activates particles for the following particle phase.
No particle banks, promotion, scheduling epochs/timestamps, custom queues or
loaded-array scheduler scans. Motion states belong inside active numerical work.

A changed centroid alone does not wake every dependent: test the combined
positional effect; a woken particle evaluates every connected field. Combine
centroid would-move flags with any-active and place each flagged centroid once,
including sleeping members. Preserve carried motion and changed-input invalidation.
For the accepted wide path, discard unpublished positional tails and retain
velocity according to the accepted #196 displacement/dt rule; do not restore
historical rounding-residue carry. Keep pre-integration contact forces and sleeping-target checks on both index sides
and across pointer blocks. Separate numerical force work from gate/adjacency and
native metadata costs. No arbitrary epsilon or zero-speed sleep rule is implied.
The [active field model](../../engine/docs/ACTIVE-FIELD-MODEL.md) owns exact predicates.

Selected focal fields define the origin by ordinary combined centroid calculation,
including the origin-dependent radial dampener. The finest required conceptual
LoD sets the floor; compatible category scope, capacity and data completeness
govern intended surrounding rollup. This is not a universal absolute 3D map or
hard Euclidean ring policy. Dynamic selection, slopes and transitions remain
unbuilt; [#91 notes](https://github.com/Human-Cognome-Project/human-cognome-project/issues/91#issuecomment-6251014344)
are not authority to invent analyst relevance rules.

Private/local information enters only at the analyst-owned active hot boundary.
The cache manager must reject private namespaces/handles **before source lookup**;
it must not read, filter, redact, publish, acquire or refresh private DB data.
Joint reports return to the active owner, never through manager reporting.
Public entity knowledge may remain shared. Use supplied private fixtures; actual
private DB access and analyst reasoning are not implemented. Earlier isolated
Stage A scoped-storage tests do not authorize a private manager path.
Warm publication, assembly completeness and execution readiness are separate.
No blanket change to temporary ownership or retained fixtures is authorized.
