# The Physics Basis

**What was determined, August 2026.** The primary sources are the two packages in this repository:
[`ledger/`](../ledger/) and [`field/`](../field/). This page is the orientation, not the substitute —
the packages carry the measurements, derivations, citations, and runnable models.

## The argument in five sentences

1. Physics welded a how-often quantity (frequency) to a how-much quantity (energy) with one
   equation, E = hν, placing the constant h at two junctions of the same formula to make the join
   hold.
2. Removing the two junctions and the square they produce — by operations that each have a named
   source — leaves the measured world on one rational axis with no time and no energy in its labels:
   the **Mann ledger** (`ledger/mann_ledger_writeup.md`, `mann_ledger_math.md`).
3. On the separated ledgers, **frequency** is only how often a sampler reads, and **amount** is only
   how much of the continuous field is present; nothing converts one into the other.
4. Treating matter as a density imbalance in that field, relaxing by diffusion, gives inverse-square
   attraction between two bodies as a product of their imbalances with no frequency term — Newton's
   law, and Coulomb's with a sign rule (`field/field_attraction_writeup.md`, run: `field/field_model.py`).
5. Every higher inverse power in physics (dipole, van der Waals, Casimir-Polder, the plate law, the
   thermal crossover) is that same inverse square compounded once per mutual adjustment folded into
   the calculation — including adjustments contributed by the field's own state.

## The principles the project runs on

**One operation.** Every process is an adaptation of **single-point force balancing**: each point
sees only its local differential and adapts toward reducing it. The optimum — a mathematical vacuum
in which every element holds the same, perfectly balanced value — is the direction of every
operation and the destination of none: **time and distance across dimensions** confound every
adjustment. Balancing is always local, always lagged, always partial.

**Identity is unfinished balancing.** A thing *is* its differential against the field. At the
teleological limit every identity is identical and perfect — which is the same as there being no
identities at all. Existence is what the confounders preserve.

**Periodicity is not inherent; it develops.** One centre in a field relaxes radially — no period
anywhere. Two or more centres cannot avoid periodicity: their emissions meet at positions fixed by
geometry and propagation rate, and the period is the timetable of the collisions, not a property of
any centre. Periodic emission is signal-equivalent (a sampler can lock to it); continuous emission is
force-like (it fills every window). Heat is the type case of the confusion this separates
(`ledger/RP_amount_ledger_rereading.md`).

**The separation rule.** Frequency belongs to samplers and lives on a log axis. Amount belongs to
the field and lives on a linear axis. Any inherited formula joining the two is cut at the join, and
any constant whose only job was the conversion is dropped. Unread is not zero: an empty slot on
either ledger stays empty until *read* — filling it by conversion is the error the procedure removes.

## What this means for a knowledge model

Knowledge elements are density imbalances in one field. Their identity, salience, and persistence
are the same measurable quantity. Associations are not stored links but standing exchange patterns
between centres, with periods set by distance and propagation time. Queries and attention are
samplers — they own their frequencies; the field owns only amounts. **Fame is frequency; contribution
is amount** — the model keeps the two ledgers apart or it merely re-derives popularity. Fully
assimilated knowledge is the heat case: present in every window, sampled as nothing, maximal as
amount.
