# From Planck to Newton Without a Weld

**The chain, in one place. Human Cognome Project, 28 August 2026.**

This note ties together four working notes produced in one session. Each stands alone; this one shows they are a single argument. Every number in them traces to a citation in `scale_map_sources.md` or to a run in `field_model.py`.

## The argument in five sentences

1. Physics welded a how-often quantity (frequency) to a how-much quantity (energy) with one equation, and placed a constant, h, at two junctions of the same formula to make the join hold.
2. Removing the two junctions and the square they produce, by operations that each have a named source, leaves the measured world on one rational axis with no time and no energy in its labels; that axis is the Mann ledger.
3. On the separated ledgers, frequency is only how often a sampler reads, and power is only how much of the continuous field is present; nothing converts one into the other.
4. Treating matter as a density imbalance in that field, relaxing by diffusion, gives the inverse-square attraction between two bodies as a product of their imbalances with no frequency term; that is Newton's law, and Coulomb's with a sign rule.
5. Every higher inverse power in physics (dipole, van der Waals, Casimir-Polder, the Casimir plate law, the thermal Casimir crossover) is that same inverse square compounded once per mutual adjustment folded into the calculation, including adjustments contributed by the field's own state.

## The chain, step by step

### Planck's two junctions (`mann_ledger_math.md`, §1–2)

u(ν,T) = (8πν²/c³) · hν / (exp(hν/kT) − 1). The ν² is the count of standing waves a box holds per frequency interval: geometry. The hν is the energy assigned per quantum: the weld. The exponential is a geometric series over occupancy with hν/kT as its step: the weld again, now bending the line. In the low-frequency limit h cancels entirely and the classical ν²·kT remains; in the high-frequency limit both copies of h show at once. Two junctions, one constant.

### The removal (`mann_ledger_writeup.md`, steps 1–5; `mann_ledger_math.md`, §3–6)

- Constants c, k, h set to 1: unit names, step change, nothing moves.
- One square root on the whole axis: removes the box's ν² from the positions. Warrant: the square is named and located. No second root is permitted.
- h applied once as a scalar: removes the second copy of h's magnitude. Step change.
- Origin declared at a round rung inside the human audible band. Named 1 Mann.

All 36 measured values keep every mutual ratio to the last digit. The ledger's shape is measurement's shape.

### The rule (`mann_ledger_writeup.md`, "The separation rule"; `mann_ledger_math.md`, §8)

Frequency: how often something occurs. Nothing more. The number belongs to the sampler. Power: how much of the field is present. Continuous, additive, linear. Any inherited formula joining the two is cut at the join; the constant that did the joining has no predicate afterward and is dropped. A square that touches all dimensions is removed only when its source is named. A step on a single linear dimension is free.

### Matter as imbalance, attraction as relaxation (`field_attraction_writeup.md`, "The model", "Results")

Two density surpluses in a field relaxing by isotropic diffusion. Steady state is Laplace's equation; point-source field falls as 1/r; its gradient as 1/r². Force on one zone = its imbalance × the other's gradient. Run result: far-field slope −2.00, product law exact, turnover to merging inside three zone widths. No frequency anywhere. This is Newton with G as the step declaration, and Coulomb with a sign rule.

### Sign as flow sense (`field_attraction_writeup.md`, "Polarity as flow")

Every body carries a continuous internal rebalancing flow in the electromagnetic band. Unlike biases present a differential to the gap; merging reduces it; the field draws them together. Like biases present none; merging would concentrate the shared bias and raise net imbalance; the field keeps them apart. One rule, two cases. A medium with its own sense between them can invert the case (predicted 1961, measured 2009). Across the field floor there is no medium and no bias, so every body is a plain surplus and gravity has one sign. What can be expressed is capped by each body's band in its medium.

### The exponent ladder (`field_attraction_writeup.md`, "The exponent ladder")

1/r² is one-way balancing. Each mutual adjustment folded into the joint result compounds it: 1/r³ for a balanced body seeing only a gradient; 1/r⁶ energy for two balanced bodies each answering the other (London); one more for propagation delay (Casimir-Polder); 1/d⁴ per area when summed over two plates (Hamaker; Lamoreaux measured); 1/d³ when the field's own thermal density takes over the interaction (measured 2011). The field's state changes the exponent, not only the coefficient. No new forces; the same square counted more times.

### Propagation (`field_attraction_writeup.md`, "Propagation")

Fick with constant diffusivity gives the mid-state: rate through a point proportional to the differential, falling as 1/r². Fick with density-dependent diffusivity (the porous-medium equation) gives a finite front whose speed is set by the magnitude arriving at it and reduces to the linear case in the interior. One law with one dependency added.

## What is open

Mechanics carried forward: periodicity is not inherent; it develops. One centre in a field (monopolar) has radial relaxation and no period anywhere. Two or more centres (polycentric) cannot avoid it: their emissions meet at positions fixed by geometry and propagation rate, and the meeting pattern is periodic. The period is the timetable of the collisions, set by the distance and propagation time between them (Kepler's third law for orbits; the cavity round trip for a bounded field). Periodic emission is signal-equivalent, a created waveform series. Continuous emission has no exchange behind it and fills every sampler window.

- **Magnitudes.** Every exponent on the ladder is fixed; no coefficient has been computed. Lamoreaux's five-percent plate number is the first target.
- **Crossover.** Which rung dominates at which separation for a body carrying both a surplus and a flow. A boundary-ratio calculation.
- **Retardation as density-dependent propagation.** Argued, not run.
- **The lower ledger.** Empty below 0.013 Mann by measurement; expected to fill.
- **Unread slots.** 24 entries with no measured amount, 12 with no measured frequency. They stay empty until read.
- **The amount side.** A first amount ledger from independent reads is built (`mann_ledger_writeup.md`, "The amount ledger"): unit 1 g water 1 K, count event the molecules in that gram; heart and breath at the unit, chemistry and heat within five decades, particles six more. Every remaining measured amount came through a sampler with a cycle, and a continuous source fills every cycle at full scale. The amount values are instrument upper bounds until re-read with the cycle stated and the source's continuity recorded. Heat is the type case (`mann_ledger_writeup.md`, "A caution on the amount side").
