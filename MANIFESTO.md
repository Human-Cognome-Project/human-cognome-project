# The Manifesto

## The problem, measured

Modern AI is statistical approximation: patterns compressed into weights, sampled probabilistically.
It is effective, and it is not comprehension — and with the project's physics basis, that critique is
no longer a stance. It is a measurement.

Statistical training **welds sampling frequency to amount**: how often a pattern occurs in the
corpus becomes how much weight it carries as truth. That is the exact inherited error the Mann
ledger procedure cuts out of physics itself (see [docs/physics-basis.md](docs/physics-basis.md)) —
E = hν applied to knowledge. Popularity-as-truth is not a side effect of the current paradigm; it is
the training objective. A system built that way cannot explain why, only that something is likely;
its errors hide in probability shadows; and it must be trusted, because it cannot be audited.

**Frequency is how often samplers read. Amount is how much is there. They are different quantities,
and any system that converts one into the other inherits every failure that follows.**

## The alternative we are building

The Human Cognome Project models all of human knowledge as one continuous field, governed by a
single operation — single-point force balancing — with every element's identity, salience, and
persistence being the same measurable quantity: its differential against the field. Frequency and
amount are kept on separate ledgers, permanently. What that buys:

- **Explainability.** Every inference is a solved flow between placed elements — traceable, and
  auditable down to the addresses.
- **No exclusion.** Everything is admitted and flagged; nothing is filtered out of consideration.
  Every problem in the world right now traces to someone deciding what to exclude from
  consideration; a differential you refuse to read does not stop exerting force.
- **Declared provenance.** Every element carries the path of compressions it came through. Nothing
  happens for free, and everything paid appears on the ledger — including our own compression.
- **Verifiability over trust.** Unread slots are marked unread, not filled by conversion. Fame and
  contribution are never confused, because they are different columns.
- **Efficiency.** One kernel, compounded, on consumer GPU hardware — not transformer inference on
  server farms.
- **Universality.** Built for all sentience — human, AI, non-human, future minds. The substrate is
  bytes and balance, not the patterns of one species.
